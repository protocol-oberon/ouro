Warning [WRN0010]: The refinement 'catalogued' is declared over value, which says nothing
  ┌── │WRN0010-RefinementOverValue.ouro:1:37│
  │
1 │ (define/refinement catalogued (<x ⊢ value>) (doc "Anything a catalogue entry may hold.") true)
  │                                     ^
  │                                     Over value: catalogued
  │ 

Every value is in value, so a refinement declared over it says nothing about its subject: its body decides everything, and nothing in its
signature tells a reader what catalogued is about. That is allowed, for a refinement that decides across kinds, but a narrower domain
says more.

Perhaps write the kinds it is about: (define/refinement catalogued (<x ⊢ data>) ...), or (<r ⊢ record>).
