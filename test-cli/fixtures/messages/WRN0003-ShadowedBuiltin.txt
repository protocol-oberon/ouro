Warning [WRN0003]: The procedure 'len' replaces the built-in 'len'
  ┌── │WRN0003-ShadowedBuiltin.ouro:1:1│
  │
1 │ (define/procedure len (x) x)
  │ ^
  │ Replaced Built-in: len
  │ 

A function named after a built-in or a special form replaces it throughout the file, as a definition
does in Scheme: a built-in in calls, in case guards and under any symbol it is also written with (=
is eq, ∪ is union), a special form wherever it is called.

Perhaps choose another name for the procedure, unless replacing 'len' is intended.
