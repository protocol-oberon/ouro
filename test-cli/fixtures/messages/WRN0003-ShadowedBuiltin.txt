  │ ^
  │ Replaced Built-in: len

A defn named after a built-in replaces it throughout the file: in calls, in case guards, and under
any symbol the built-in is also written with (= is eq, ∪ is union).

Perhaps choose another name for the function, unless replacing 'len' is intended.
